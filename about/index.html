01 — Who we are

Principle Adhesives Pvt. Ltd.

We are amongst the leading manufacturers of adhesives and sealants in India. We are based out of Mumbai, having clientele all over India.

Our background

All our product offerings are driven by three decades of relentless research, innovation, and market orientation. Our long-term patronage with our clients in India has helped us in cementing our position as one of the leading manufacturer & supplier of next-generation products.

Technology-driven

With the constant endeavor to partner with our clients and understand newer bonding requirements & challenges has transformed Principle Adhesives into a technology driven powerhouse manufacturing unit.

Trusted by industry

Principle Adhesives Pvt Ltd has played a pivotal role in handling multiple challenges faced by some of the biggest manufacturers of automobiles, mattresses, furniture, and paper products in India today and delivered the desired products.

Products and markets

Our meticulously formulated and comprehensive range of Synthetic Rubber, Polyurethane, PVA and PVC based adhesives are available in water and solvent based formulations. We cater to all sections of industries like Furniture, Mattresses, Footwear, Automobiles, Paper & Packaging, Flooring/Carpet, PVC Pipes & Fittings, and various others. Apart from enjoying a pan India presence, our products are significantly reaching the global markets.
